LightLine menu bar: design

A VS Code-style menu bar in LightLine's title bar, between the logo and the search box. Every item runs a command LightLine already has (most are Command Palette commands), and shows its shortcut. Drawn in LightLine's own colors and in the style of its existing "..." and right-click menus.

Click a menu title, then hover to switch menus. Hold Alt to see access keys, then press a letter (F, E, S, V, G, R, T, H). Arrow keys move, Enter runs, Esc closes.

Behaviour

  • Mouse: a click on a title opens its menu; while one is open, moving onto another title opens that one; a click outside, or on the open title, closes it.
  • Keyboard: Alt+F, Alt+E, Alt+S, Alt+V, Alt+G, Alt+R, Alt+T, Alt+H open a menu; holding Alt underlines the access letters; Up/Down move, Left/Right go to the next menu, Enter runs, Esc closes. Alt+R still toggles Regular Expression while the find box has focus.
  • Commands: each item calls what the Command Palette or its shortcut already calls, so nothing behaves differently from a menu. Items that don't apply right now (Run with no file open) are greyed out.
  • Speed: painted in the backbuffer like the other menus; nothing runs until a menu opens, no timers, no new work at startup. The titles are part of the chrome, redrawn only on full-frame paints, so typing and scrolling stay on the fast path.
